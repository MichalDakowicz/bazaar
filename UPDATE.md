# Update notes

## 0.1.0 — Unreleased

### Added

- Lists: make several lists, each with a shop and a day, and see how far through each one is
- List: swipe an item to put it in the basket, tap it in the basket to put it back, Undo on every tick
- Add: search in English or Polish; flour, eggs, milk and more ask the follow-up a shop would
- Add: words like "mąka do pierogów" pick the options for you and say which word they heard
- Catalog: about a thousand products in ten shop sections, each named in both languages
- Catalog: your usuals come back as exactly what you bought, one tap
- Web: a planner with the catalog, the open list and a keyboard-first search
- Recipe: paste a recipe and see what the list already covers, what needs more and what is new
- Household: add a friend to a list and see what they add, live
- Household: when someone starts shopping, watch their progress and add last-minute items
- History: every finished trip, with Reuse
- Settings: app language and product-name language, swipe to check, notices, theme
- Sign-in: continue with a Ping app already signed in on this phone, or scan a QR code
